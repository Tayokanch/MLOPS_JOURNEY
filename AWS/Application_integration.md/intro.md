# APPLICATION INTEGRATION IN AWS

- When we say Application Integration, we're talking about something that is going to manipulate the **traffic** or **Communication** btw Applications.

- Application Integration is about managing the **flow** , **quality**, **buffering** the speed, and the rate of communcation btw two applications or an application to a person.

- Its about creating queues or lines in a sense, and manipulating the flow of traffic.
